Re-Architecting the Enrollment Pipeline: A 12-Month Case Study in Enterprise Information Architecture and Conversion Velocity

Kel-Lee, M.S. CSIT
UX Content Architect & Web Strategist • Forsyth Technical Community College
Abstract
Modern higher education web ecosystems frequently suffer from a structural paradox: the necessity of housing extensive, compliance-mandated curriculum repositories versus the strategic imperative of providing a frictionless, conversion-focused user journey. This paper examines a 12-month user experience (UX) engineering initiative executed at Forsyth Technical Community College. By implementing a modular, semantic template system, the root academic directory was safely scaled by 174 enterprise pages to capture highly specific, long-tail search intent. Concurrently, high-intent onboarding funnels were flattened to mitigate cognitive fatigue. Through continuous product iteration (1,200+ technical web revisions) and rigorous analytics optimization in Google Analytics 4 (GA4), the project achieved an asymmetric performance lift: a +45.91% surge in user volume alongside an intentional -34.87% reduction in layout processing time. This study establishes a data-validated framework for executing large-scale information architecture (IA) updates without degrading site navigation mechanics.

1. Problem Statement & Operational Context

Prospective community college students exhibit highly fragmented search patterns, frequently querying specific, localized certification pathways rather than generalized degree titles. However, nearly half of Forsyth Tech’s specialized academic offerings historically remained offline, buried inside desktop-bound documents, or obscured within dense, text-heavy directory files. This introduced two critical business vulnerabilities:

  • Discovery Deficit: The lack of indexable, search-engine-optimized sub-pages meant the institution failed to capture valuable organic search impressions from non-traditional or continuing education candidates.
  • Cognitive Overwhelm: When users successfully located enrollment prerequisites, they encountered unstructured "walls of text." This increased cognitive friction, resulting in prolonged session durations characterized by disorientation rather than productive form completion.

The strategic mandate was to scale the institutional digital footprint to surface all offline curriculum modules while simultaneously engineering a high-velocity conversion funnel to accelerate application submissions.

2. Methodology & Architectural Interventions

2.1 Scalable Information Architecture (IA) Design

To prevent system-wide layout fragmentation, I designed a rigid, component-driven layout matrix inside the Content Management System (CMS). This allowed the digital ecosystem to absorb a massive volume of new content while preserving a unified user experience. Over the annual development cycle, this framework supported the programmatic deployment of 174 brand-new web environments:

  • 20 Foundational Program Hubs: Established high-level landing categories (/programs-courses/) designed to anchor core academic divisions and provide macro-level navigation options.
  • 124 Specialized Program Track Pages: Deployed highly focused, granular certification sub-pages, successfully migrating 48.2% of the institution's localized curriculum catalog directly onto the indexable web.
  • 30 Ecosystem Support Pages: Built critical auxiliary routing nodes—including specialized landing pages for financial aid protocols, enrollment checklist tracking, and student service assistance.

2.2 Institutional Technical SEO Strategy

A major web expansion is only as effective as its discoverability index. A comprehensive semantic and technical SEO overhaul was woven into the page deployment layer: Standardized strict header patterns (H1 through H4) to ensure search engines could easily parse the relational hierarchy between parent programs and specialized sub-tracks. Concurrently, line heights, target click parameters, and structural CSS properties were optimized to eliminate layout shifting across mobile devices, while meta logs were aligned to catch localized continuing-education searches.

2.3 Agile Product Engineering & Maintenance Operations

Deploying an enterprise-grade content framework requires an aggressive, continuous optimization model to ingest user feedback, satisfy cross-departmental compliance checks, and preserve asset health. By establishing an optimized operational rhythm, I successfully managed over 1,200 verified technical web revisions across the annual timeline. Production analytics from the Q1 cycle demonstrate an exceptionally high operational velocity—sustaining 151 revisions in February and 126 revisions in March alone, while compressing the institutional Service Level Agreement (SLA) down to a strict 1–2 week time-to-publish turnaround.

3. Data Analysis & Behavioral Outcomes

Ecosystem behavioral data was compiled using comparative, year-over-year (YoY) multi-channel tracking. The findings reveal a profound shift in user interaction models across both desktop and mobile viewports.

3.1 Content Retention and Interest Capture

Rather than diluting traffic profiles or spiking exit vectors, the massive addition of content stabilized and enhanced the main educational hub. The primary directory path (/programs-courses/) experienced a +6.52% traffic growth on desktop (reaching 39,967 active users) and a +6.72% growth on mobile (reaching 34,401 active users). More importantly, the new track pages held user interest cleanly, keeping average session interaction lengths exceptionally high at 1 minute and 10 seconds on desktop and 57 seconds on mobile viewports, maintaining an active baseline engagement rate above 91%.

3.2 Desktop Funnel Velocity & Cognitive Load Mitigation

The most definitive architectural validation emerged from the primary onboarding node (/steps-to-apply/). By replacing text-heavy lists with a highly scannable, flat, visual checklist grid, user interaction dynamics shifted dramatically. As technical SEO optimization funneled an influx of new traffic to the page (+45.91% user growth, totaling 2,428 active desktop users), the average time spent on the page decreased by -34.87%.

In standard digital marketing, a reduction in time-on-page is misinterpreted as a failure indicator. However, in complex UX product funnels, this represents an extraordinary victory in cognitive load reduction. By presenting dense application requirements in an optimized layout, prospective students parsed the technical information instantly and transitioned straight into active application profiles (/apply-now/ sessions expanded to 1 minute and 15 seconds of highly focused form-field completion).

3.3 Mobile Onboarding Metrics

Concurrently, on mobile devices where reading multi-step layouts historically caused severe drop-offs, the typography and layout adjustments successfully held user focus. Mobile user volume on onboarding pathways expanded by +15.40%, while the interaction length expanded by +44.16% YoY, extending sessions past the critical mobile bounce threshold.

4. Telemetry Integrity & Advanced Data-Cleaning

To ensure that all optimization trends were true reflections of prospective student behavior rather than network interference, a rigorous data hygiene pass was conducted in the final stages of the annual audit. Top-level analytics initially showed an unexpected, un-segmented 40,000-user traffic surge entering the root directory via direct desktop channels, recording artificial 13-second session durations.

By executing advanced telemetry segmentation inside GA4, I identified this anomaly as automated non-regional data center bot scrapers. I constructed strict, geo-fenced proxy filters to isolate traffic to regional recruitment markets. This technical pass excluded over 35,000 automated non-human sessions, successfully isolating an authentic baseline of 14,363 active direct users. This data-cleaning pass verified with absolute certainty that the global engagement growth (+42.76% deep reading extension on desktop organic search) was entirely driven by real human applicants interacting with the newly launched pages.

5. Conclusions & Next-Cycle Product Roadmap

This case study demonstrates that large-scale information architecture expansion, when paired with component-driven design and structured SEO templates, can scale an enterprise web footprint without fragmenting the user journey. By framing data-cleaning as a crucial quality assurance layer, the project successfully isolated and verified genuine human behavioral improvements. The audited 12-month performance baselines have unlocked a clear data-backed roadmap for the next UX engineering sprint:

  • Programmatic Path Cross-Linking: Automatically injecting top-performing short-term vocational tracks (/fast-track-courses/, which drew 12,837 mobile users this year) directly into the contextual sidebars of the newly deployed 124 program track pages to capture cross-categorical interest.
  • Progressive Onboarding Disclosures: Leveraging the high reading velocity on desktop checklists to introduce interactive, automated progress bars that turn fast visual patterns into immediate enrollment form submissions.
